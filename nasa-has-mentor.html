LEADERSHIP / CASE STUDY

NASA High School Aerospace Scholars

Supporting eight students through feedback, communication, and proactive mentorship.

ORGANIZATION
NASA High School Aerospace Scholars
ROLE / STATUS
Senior Mentor · Oct 2024 – Apr 2025
FOCUS
Mentorship · Feedback · Student support

01 / OVERVIEW

Returning as a mentor

After participating in NASA’s High School Aerospace Scholars (HAS) program, I returned as a Senior Mentor, supporting eight students as they worked through their coursework.

I graded submitted assignments, provided constructive feedback, and served as a point of communication between my students and our senior advisor. At the same time, I worked with fellow peer mentors on our own STEM-based coursework.

02 / RECOGNIZING THE PROBLEM

Recognizing when students needed more

Later in the semester, I began noticing missed deadlines and declining grades among my students. I realized that grading assignments and providing feedback alone wasn't always enough to help them keep up.

03 / TAKING INITIATIVE

Creating space for support

I organized monthly meetings with my students to discuss their progress, encourage them, and offer practical advice on managing their time and responsibilities.

Rather than waiting for someone to fall further behind or reach out for help, I wanted to make support more accessible and consistent.

04 / IMPACT

Seeing improvement

After I introduced the meetings, I noticed improvements in my students’ academic performance and overall progress.

The experience reinforced the importance of recognizing when people need support and taking action rather than waiting for them to ask.

05 / REFLECTION

Leadership through encouragement

Mentorship taught me that helping someone succeed involves more than evaluating their work. It requires paying attention, communicating clearly, and recognizing when your approach needs to change.

I came to appreciate that encouragement can be just as important as instruction. A good mentor doesn't simply help someone complete an assignment; they help that person become more confident and capable of succeeding independently.
