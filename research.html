01 / RESEARCH INTERESTS

I’m interested in understanding why systems work, where their limits are, and how thoughtful design can improve them. My interests are still evolving as I build my engineering foundation.

I’m especially drawn to aerospace propulsion and flight systems, as well as the interaction between electrical and mechanical systems.
