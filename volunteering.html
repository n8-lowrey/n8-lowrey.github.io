NATHAN LOWREY / ENGINEERING PORTFOLIO

Volunteering

Using my time and abilities to serve others, support my community, and grow along the way.

01 / WHY I SERVE

Service with purpose

My faith has played a significant role in shaping how I approach serving others. I believe that the time, skills, and opportunities I've been given are meant to be used for more than just my own benefit.

Whether I'm helping children learn, supporting my community, or playing drums at church, volunteering gives me an opportunity to contribute in different ways. Some experiences have challenged me to develop patience and leadership, while others have allowed me to share something I genuinely enjoy.

For me, service isn't just about giving my time. It's about finding ways to use what I have to make a positive difference in the lives of others.

02 / YOUTH EDUCATION

Church Teaching Assistant

Weekly volunteer service · Fall 2022 – Present

I help prepare lesson materials and support classes for children in kindergarten through sixth grade. During lessons, I work directly with students, explain activities, distribute and collect materials, and help keep the classroom engaged.

Working with children who learn and focus in different ways has taught me patience and flexibility. I've learned to explain the same idea in several ways and, most importantly, to use encouragement to help students build confidence.

03 / YOUTH CAMPS

Summer Camp Counselor

Church summer camps · Recurring volunteer service

At summer camp, I help supervise groups of children, lead activities, and support lessons throughout the program. The role calls for attentiveness, teamwork, and the ability to make children feel welcome and included.

It's another opportunity to put patience and encouragement into practice while helping create a positive experience for younger students.

04 / MUSIC & WORSHIP

Church Band Drummer

Volunteer musician

Drumming began with my love for music. Playing with my church band gives me a way to share something I genuinely enjoy while contributing to worship services.

It has also taught me the value of listening, preparation, and working in sync with a group—skills that matter as much offstage as they do behind a drum kit.

05 / COMMUNITY SERVICE

BETA Club

Community service activities

Through BETA Club, I participated in service efforts including work with 4B Disaster Response, support for Inspira Pregnancy Center, and community cleanups.

These opportunities showed me how much of a difference people can make when they work together to meet practical needs in their communities.

06 / UNIVERSITY SERVICE

Lamar University Career Fair Volunteer

I volunteered to support a Lamar University career fair, contributing to an event that brought students and employers together. It was an opportunity to serve my university community while seeing firsthand the importance of professional connections.
